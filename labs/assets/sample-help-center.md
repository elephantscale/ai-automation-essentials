# Sample Help Center — Northwind Software (fictional)

A short, fictional help-center document for classroom use. Ground a custom assistant in
this file so it answers **only** from these articles — and says "I don't know, let me
route you to a person" when a question isn't covered here. Everything below is made up.

---

## Shipping & Delivery

- Standard delivery of physical license kits is 3–5 business days within the continental US.
- We do not currently ship physical kits outside the US; international customers receive
  license keys by email only.
- Tracking links are emailed automatically once an order ships.

## Returns & Refunds

- Software subscriptions can be cancelled any time; access continues until the end of the
  paid period. We do not prorate partial months.
- A refund is available within **14 days** of the first purchase of a new annual plan.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
- Downgrade/upgrade takes effect on the next billing cycle.

## Billing

- We accept major credit cards and ACH for annual plans over $2,000.
- Invoices are available under Account → Billing → Invoices.
- A failed payment retries automatically for 7 days before the account is suspended.
- To update a card, go to Account → Billing → Payment methods.

## Accounts & Login

- Reset a password from the sign-in page using "Forgot password."
- Admins can add or remove team members under Account → Team.
- Single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.

## Technical Support

- Known outages and status are posted at status.northwind.example (fictional).
- For a bug, include your account email, the steps to reproduce, and a screenshot.
- Priority (same-business-day) response is included on Business and Enterprise plans.

---

> **Intentionally NOT covered here** (use these to test the "I don't know / escalate"
> behavior): HIPAA or handling of patient data, legal/contract questions, custom on-prem
> deployment, anything about a specific customer's account balance, and pricing for plans
> above Enterprise. A well-grounded assistant should refuse to answer these from this
> document and route them to a human.
