# AI Automation Essentials for Business Professionals

*Build AI into your real workflows — no code required.*

© Elephant Scale

## Course Description

A two-day, hands-on course for non-technical professionals, team leads, and
decision-makers. You get fluent in generative AI, master prompt engineering for
automation, learn to evaluate and troubleshoot AI output, build no-code AI workflows and a
simple AI agent, use AI for decisions and insights, apply a responsible-AI/governance
framework (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF), and calculate ROI — ending in a capstone where you
design, build, and present an automation for a real challenge from your own work.

## Audience

Business professionals, team leads, and managers in HR, finance, operations, marketing, or
support who want to apply AI confidently, safely, and productively. No coding required.

## Duration

2 Days / 16 hours. Introductory to Intermediate. Hands-on, exercise-driven.

- A no-code automation platform (Zapier, Make, or Power Automate) — matched to the client
- No local software installation and no coding required
