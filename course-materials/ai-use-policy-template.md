## 2. Data rules (what never goes into a prompt)

- Never paste: passwords/keys, customer personal data, payment/bank numbers, health or
  other regulated data, unreleased financials/legal matters, source/records from systems
  you don't own.
- When in doubt: redact, or ask __________ before pasting.

## 3. Human-in-the-loop

- A person must review before any AI-assisted action that **sends, publishes, deletes,
  buys, or changes a record.**
- High-stakes decisions (hiring, credit, legal, medical, safety) are **decision support
  only** — a human decides.

## 4. Verification

- Verify facts, numbers, dates, and citations before reuse.
- Keep the prompt + output when the work must be repeatable or auditable.
- For file-based work, record which files were used and ask for row, section, or quote evidence.
- For connected-app or connector workflows, confirm least-privilege access and human review
  before enabling write-back or external send actions.
